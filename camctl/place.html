Which way is it pointing

Range is where it can still read a plate, not where it can see a car. At a webcam's field of view that is roughly 10–15 m. Overstating it puts sightings in the wrong place on the map.

Name

The name and an approximate position are public. RavenMap jitters the published position by up to 60 m so the map shows where you are recorded without publishing which house is watching.
