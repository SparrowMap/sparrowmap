This is a software crop, not the camera's zoom. The C920's own zoom is digital too, so it crops the same sensor and then upscales — which adds pixels and no detail. Cropping here keeps the real pixels and works on every camera, including fixed ones with no PTZ at all.

Setting exposure or gain switches the camera to manual exposure. That is what you want for a fixed node: auto exposure chases headlights at night and blows out the plate, which is the one thing in frame that matters.
