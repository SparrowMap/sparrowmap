Help train the detector

What kind of vehicle is this? About two seconds each.

Why these ones. The detector already looked at every picture here and got it wrong, or was not sure. Those are the only images worth a person's time, so this queue is the mistakes rather than a random pile.

What happens to your answer. It trains the detector. It does not put anything on the map, and it cannot: nothing here can publish a vehicle. Several people see each picture and the answers are compared before any of it is used.

What you are looking at. Every image comes from a public traffic camera, never from a volunteer's own camera, and carries no location, no time and no number plate.

Part of RavenMap, a camera network the neighbourhood owns. Government vehicles are public and searchable; everybody else is hashed at the camera and deleted. How it works · What it costs
