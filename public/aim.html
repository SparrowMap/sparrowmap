This page points a camera at the road it actually watches.

Open it from the device that registered the camera and it finds the key by itself. Otherwise paste the camera token you were given when you registered it.

This camera is registered as carried (driving mode). A carried camera has no watched road, so there is nothing to aim — its sightings carry their own position.

Drag from the camera toward the road it watches. That sets the direction and how far it can see, in one movement.

Off by default, and off is a fine choice. Your camera's own position is never published either way, and turning this off does not stop the camera contributing sightings.

Why turning it on helps: it is the honest half of the project. Anyone can see exactly which stretch of road is being recorded, and therefore where they are not. A map of patrols with no way to check where the watching happens asks people to take it on trust.

Why you might leave it off: the stretch is a real, named piece of road. In a town that tells nobody much. On a quiet road where yours is the only house overlooking it, the road plus its name can point at your home. If that describes where you are, leave this off.
