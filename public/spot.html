Report a surveillance camera 📷

See a Flock or ALPR camera on a pole? Photograph it right from your browser. No app, no sign-in.

How this stays honest: a photo is required, and every report waits for a person to look at it before anything appears on the map. A report is a claim, never a marker. Nothing you send is published automatically.

Only the photo you take and the spot's location are sent. Your identity is not.
