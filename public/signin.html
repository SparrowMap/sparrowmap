Where is my key? It is the link you copied from the camera page (Copy the key link), the QR you saved, or the key file on a USB stick. Anyone who has it has the camera, so keep it somewhere only you can reach.
Saved one of those to this phone? Use Use a saved key file or QR photo above rather than typing it out — it reads the key file, a photo of the QR, or a note you pasted it into.

Really lost it? There is no reset — the key is the only credential and we hold no email or account to prove it was yours. Set the camera up again from the camera page and it will start a fresh node. Its old sightings stay on the map under the old camera.

Lost your reviewer token? You do not need it. Sign in with your camera key above and this page sets your review queue up for you — it issues a fresh token and retires the old one. A reviewer token is stored scrambled, so nobody (us included) can ever read your old one back to you; a new one is the only way, and it takes no extra step.

Have the reviewer token itself? Paste it in the same box — it works too.
