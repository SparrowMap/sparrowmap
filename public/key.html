Your camera key

An account without an account.

No camera on this device.

Register one on the camera page first, or scan a key from another device.

Photograph this with another device to give it control of this camera — live view, aim, rename. No sign-in, no email, nothing to remember.

Whoever holds this key controls the camera. That is what makes it anonymous — there is no account to tie it to a person, so there is nothing to prove and nothing to take. It also means a photograph of this screen is enough. Treat it like a house key, not a password.

Lost it, or showed it to the wrong person? Rotate below. Every copy of the old key stops working immediately.

If the key gets out

Mints a new key for the same camera. The camera keeps its history and its place on the map; only the key changes. Any device still holding the old one loses access at once — including this one, unless you re-save the new key here.

What a key is

It is a web address with a secret on the end, and the secret sits after a #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so scanning a key transmits nothing to us or to anyone in between. The capability goes from the picture into the device and stops there.

We cannot recover it for you, because we do not have it.
